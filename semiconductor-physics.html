Supplementary short lessons

  1. Reading a Semiconductor Band Diagram

    Find the lowest electronic excitation, then ask what a photon can reach without borrowing momentum from the lattice.

  2. Why Wavevectors Fold Back

    Use translation symmetry to put wavevectors in one zone, then distinguish a folded free-electron crossing from a true gap.

  3. How many electrons fit a band?

    Count the available states in three dimensions, then use the Fermi level to find how many of those states contain electrons or holes.

  4. From band curvature to current

    Use the curvature of a band to find how carriers accelerate, then keep particle motion and conventional current separate when drift and diffusion compete.
