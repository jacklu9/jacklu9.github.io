Write Ec for the lowest conduction energy and Ev for the highest valence energy. The labels c and
v mean conduction and valence. The fundamental band gap is
Eg = Ec −Ev,
g means gap.
Here the black valleys at X, Γ and L have energies 1.04, 1.92 and 1.22 eV. Comparing all three
selects Ec = 1.04 eV. The blue maximum gives Ev = 0. A valley is a neighbourhood of a minimum
on an energy curve. A local minimum is lowest among nearby states; the global minimum is lowest
over the full band.
The two gap-defining dots need not share a horizontal address. That is the unresolved question for
light: may an optical excitation change this address, or must it keep the endpoints on one vertical
line?

Why a crystal offers bands of possible states
A state is one allowed quantum description of an electron: its band, spatial wave pattern and spin
label. Spin has two possible projections; it is an intrinsic property, not a tiny classical orbit. An
available state need not be occupied:
