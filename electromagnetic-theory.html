Supplementary short lessons

  1. Vectors, gradients, and flux

    An arrow at a point, a direction of change, and a field crossing a surface answer different questions.

  2. Divergence, curl, and the integral theorems

    Two local operators, two orientations, and a field for which both laws can be checked by hand.

  3. A current without charge crossing the gap

    Derive charge conservation, then use two surfaces on the same loop to see why a charging capacitor needs the displacement term.

  4. Phasors and what can jump at an interface

    Keep frequency and phase explicit, then use a pillbox and a shrinking loop to see which field components can change at a material boundary.
