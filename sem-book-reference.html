Quick checks.
Band slope sets the field sign; EF −Ei sets carrier abundance; charge sets the
signs of potential energy and force; equilibrium requires cancellation of drift and diffusion. Equal
concentrations do not imply zero field: in (a), n = p at x = L/2 while E > 0.

Symbol reference
The symbols below collect the meanings introduced along the way. A reused letter is identified by
its physical context and units.
Symbol
Meaning
E, Ec, Ev
Electron energy, conduction-band edge, valence-band edge.
Eg = Ec −Ev
Fundamental band gap.
k, k
Wavevector and a scalar wavevector coordinate or magnitude, as specified locally; not
position.
kB
Boltzmann constant; distinguished from wave number k.
m0, m∗
Free-electron mass and effective mass in a band.
g(E), f(E)
Available states per volume per energy; probability of electron occupation.
EF , Ei
Fermi level; intrinsic Fermi level at the same temperature.
n, p, ni
Electron, hole and intrinsic carrier concentrations.
ND, NA
Donor and acceptor concentrations.
e > 0
Elementary charge; electron charge −e, hole charge +e.
V , E
Electrostatic potential and electric field: E = −dV /dx.
µ, τ, D
Mobility, momentum-relaxation time and diffusion coeﬀicient.
J, I
Conventional current density and current, with I = JA for uniform J.
ρch, ρ
Charge density and electrical resistivity, respectively.
In the state-counting derivation, V denotes sample volume; in electrostatics it denotes potential,
with the units making the distinction explicit.
The function exp(u) = eu uses the mathematical number e ≃2.718, whereas a charge factor such
as en uses the elementary charge. The argument u of an exponential, logarithm or trigonometric
function must be dimensionless.
The symbol p also denotes momentum in the wave–particle formulas; elsewhere it denotes hole
concentration. Its meaning is fixed by the formula and units.
At 300 K, the rounded thermal energy used in the worked problems is kBT ≃0.026 eV = 26 meV;
the thermal voltage is kBT/e ≃0.026 V = 26 mV.
The worked-example navigator gives direct routes back to the calculations. The detailed contents
at the end follows the reading path through the chapter.
