1.2.5
Faraday’s law
Charge balance explains how sources can change. It has not yet told us how a changing magnetic
ﬁeld constrains the electric ﬁeld around it. This is where the loop viewpoint earns its place: the
relevant electric measurement is circulation, rather than outward ﬂux.
